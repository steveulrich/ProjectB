# Ring energy reconstruction

The four ribbon modules are new geometry derived from recovered ring gaps. They are not original Breakaway meshes. Their placement uses the recovered energy emitter's complete parent transform.

## Evidence and interpretation

The recovered particle library is preserved at `Sources/Effects/lvl_eldorado_seattle_a.xml`. `CenterRing.MagnetRing_Energy` and its five `Gap_` containers have zero alpha. They organize debris emitters and do not supply visible ribbon geometry. The source also contains violet `Mag_Ring_01` and `Mag_Ring_02` materials, but no corresponding visible ribbon CGF placement was found.

The LOD0 ring meshes produce four empty angular intervals in the energy emitter's local XZ plane:

| Module | Measured angular interval | Corresponding source marker |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `SM_Dorado_RingEnergy_Gap_01` | 15°–39° | `Gap_04` |
| `SM_Dorado_RingEnergy_Gap_02` | 103.5°–122° | `Gap_01` |
| `SM_Dorado_RingEnergy_Gap_03` | 208.5°–231.5° | `Gap_02` |
| `SM_Dorado_RingEnergy_Gap_04` | 290.5°–317° | `Gap_03` |

Angles use `atan2(z, x)`. The measurement bins triangles at 0.5° resolution within a source radial band of 38–55 m. `Gap_05` lies on occupied geometry; it does not justify an extra ribbon.

Each module uses three thin curved strands at the measured median radius of 45.37199 source meters. Strand width, depth spacing, a small deterministic wave, and half-degree endpoint overlap are reconstruction choices. The module kit totals 576 triangles.

## Regenerate the source assets

The following files preserve the editable construction:

- `Config/ring_source_transforms.json`: recovered emitter world transforms.
- `Config/ring_energy_geometry.json`: measured gaps, marker correspondence, and transform.
- `Scripts/analyze_ring_gaps.py`: repeatable gap measurement from the owned Collada files.
- `Scripts/build_ring_energy.py`: Blender mesh and FBX generation.
- `Dorado_RingEnergy_Reconstructed.blend`: editable modules at source-derived placement.
- `energy_manifest.json`: native-import material slots and instance transforms.

Run `analyze_ring_gaps.py` with system Python, then run `build_ring_energy.py` in Blender background mode with factory startup. These scripts preserve the main recovered scene and export only the four owned energy modules. Blender 5.2.3 LTS successfully generated the current source package.

## Native material and assembly

Use the native Energy master with source material ID `mag_ring_01_matgroup__1__mag_ring_02`. The manifest provides a stable short FBX slot name and its full material-ID mapping.

The source appearance has violet diffuse color, lavender emission, opacity 0.3, and a flowing river texture with U tiling of 5. For an unlit Unreal material, connect the tinted texture to emission; an unused base-color input cannot illuminate unlit geometry. Combine source alpha with a transverse UV fade to soften strand edges, and expose flow speed and emission strength.

Import the FBXs at their calibrated centimeter scale. Apply the manifest's location, quaternion, and scale once. The quaternion includes the established source-to-Unreal Y reflection. Disable collision, navigation influence, and shadow casting on these decorative modules.

The source export completed successfully. Native import, transparency, looping motion, endpoint alignment, and comparison-camera appearance require the root editor validation report.

## Pedestal and ring glow cores

`Config/inferred_glow_points.json` records 14 small native primitive placements generated by `Scripts/prepare_glow_points.py`. Six pedestal cores preserve the exact original `PE_MagnetLight_01` through `06` origins in `vfx_a.lyr`; the source `PinLight_Alpha` and `PinLight_Add` child emitters have no local translation. Four ring emitter centers (`01`, `02`, `06`, and `07`) are clearly supported by the supplied reference images. Each has a small core on both ring faces, positioned from the source LOD0 surface and inherited particle transform. The three other below-arena emitters are omitted because their visible contribution is not established by the references.

These spheres are reconstructed static surrogates for particle glow, not recovered original mesh assets or complete particle simulations. The 44 cm pedestal diameter and 22 by 6 by 22 cm ring-core dimensions are conservative visual estimates. The source effect IDs, matrices, child offsets, matching geometry IDs, surface measurements, and evidence are retained in the manifest. Apply the owned violet emission material only to these new cores. Disable collision, navigation influence, and shadows; no point lights are prescribed. Inspect the resulting core size, bloom, and seating in the existing cavities before acceptance.
