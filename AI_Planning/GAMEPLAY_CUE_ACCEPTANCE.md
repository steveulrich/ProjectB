## 2. Production Architecture & Invariants

- **Cold-Startup Cue Registration (`BwayGameplayCueTags`):**
  - Registers native GameplayCue tags in the primary module dictionary before `UGameplayCueManager` initializes, eliminating cold-startup unregistered-tag warnings across packaged builds.
- **Server-Confirmed HitConfirm & Local Feedback Guard (`UBwayCombatFeedbackRouterComponent` & `UGCN_BwayCombatFeedback`):**
  - `UBwayCombatFeedbackRouterComponent` dispatches `GameplayCue.Breakaway.Combat.HitConfirm` on authority using a scoped empty `FPredictionKey` so `NetMulticast_InvokeGameplayCueExecuted_WithParams` reaches predicting clients without being dropped as a duplicate prediction.
  - `UGCN_BwayCombatFeedback` checks `Pawn->IsLocallyControlled()` before triggering camera shakes or controller force feedback, preventing remote hits from shaking non-owning local viewports.
- **Relic State Audio Multicast (`ARelicActor::Multicast_PlayStateAudio`):**
  - Fires an unreliable net multicast carrying a resolved `USoundBase*` and `FVector_NetQuantize` only when `CurrentState != PreviousState` and `bPlayTransitionAudio` is true (suppressing false audio on initial spawn and round-reset neutral placement).
- **Optional action, structure, zone, and status lifetimes (build5):**
  - [Base ability presentation](../Plugins/GameFeatures/BreakawayCore/Source/BreakawayCoreRuntime/Private/Abilities/BwayGameplayAbility_Base.cpp) starts configured cast/loop cues on authority only after successful commit. End/cancel removes its loop; ability removal suppresses an end burst. Existing native cast routes can leave these optional bindings unset.
  - [Shared presentation state](../Plugins/GameFeatures/BreakawayCore/Source/BreakawayCoreRuntime/Private/Combat/BwayCuePresentationComponent.cpp) reference-counts overlapping helper-owned loops and preserves preexisting external cues. Sun Shrine, Cursed Ward, and Jail use stable cosmetic attachment roots and optional noncolliding meshes. Cleanup removes local visuals without changing gameplay collision.
  - Blessing and Spite zone actors own their active loops through destruction; late observers receive current activity without a historical start burst. Duration Gameplay Effects own slowed, silenced, rooted, armor-shred, and damage-amplified cues. Saved bindings passed cold readback; complete replicated presentation still requires verification.
- **Fractional healing (build5):**
  - Routing preserves finite positive raw amounts while match stats retain integer rounding. Number text uses at most one decimal and displays positive amounts below `0.1` as `<0.1`. Formatter and complete local-routing regressions passed in build6, including raw/reflected `0.4`, tiny amounts, invalid inputs, integer API compatibility, and heal-only feedback.
